💻 Web Development

Learn how websites are planned, built, and brought to life in a browser. Start with the foundations, then put them together in responsive pages of your own.

Explore topics like

  • HTML structure and accessible content
  • CSS styling, layouts, and responsive design
  • JavaScript fundamentals and interactivity

📊 Data Science

Discover how to turn raw data into useful insights. Practice asking questions, exploring datasets, and communicating what the results mean.

Explore topics like

  • Data preparation and exploratory analysis
  • Charts, visualization, and communicating findings
  • Introduction to statistics and machine learning

📣 Digital Marketing

Learn how organizations reach and engage audiences online. Explore practical ways to build a digital presence and measure how well a campaign is working.

Explore topics like

  • Search engine optimization (SEO)
  • Social media and content strategy
  • Online branding and campaign analytics
